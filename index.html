Price vs Volume — Is It Proportional?

If price is proportional to volume, every size gives the same price per mL. In real markets, bigger containers often give a better unit rate — bulk is cheaper. Edit the prices below and watch the unit-rate bars. Are they all the same height? If yes, price and volume are proportional. If not, some sizes are better deals than others.
